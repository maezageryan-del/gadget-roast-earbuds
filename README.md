One line roasting a gadget used every day, for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-daring-gecko-25-one-line-roast-the-gadget-you-use-3d6aa610) bounty **"One-Line Roast: The Gadget You Use Every Day"**.

## The roast

> My earbuds connect to my neighbour's TV faster than to my own phone - they have a better social life than I do.

111 characters, one line, nothing else. The earbuds pair with the wrong
device before the right one — the gadget's misbehaviour is the joke, and
the "better social life" turn is the sting. At 111 bytes it sits at the
short end of the field, which also wins the size tie-break.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes that exact line to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`). No network, no
randomness — two runs produce byte-identical output.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
